About FLORA

What is FLORA?

FLORA is a fashion-tech concept: you describe clothing in natural language, and a generative workflow turns that description into a visual outfit concept. This site is a static front-end demonstration — it shows how an AI garment studio could feel in product form, from prompt to collection.

01

Describe

Write the silhouette, fabric, mood, and details you want in plain words.

02

Generate

The studio interprets your prompt and presents an editorial concept look.

03

Refine

Adjust style, fit, color, and occasion — then regenerate until it resonates.

04

Wear

Save favorites to your collection and return to create the next piece.
